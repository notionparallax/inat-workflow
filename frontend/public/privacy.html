Privacy

Dive iNat and the Dive iNat frame grabber extension. Last updated 18 November 2026.

The short version

Dive iNat has no server and no accounts of its own. It collects nothing. Everything happens in your browser, and the only places your data goes are YouTube (to play the video) and iNaturalist (when you choose to upload).

The website

  • Your iNaturalist API token is kept in your browser's local storage and sent only to api.inaturalist.org. It expires after 24 hours.
  • Your dive log is read in your browser. The parts the tool uses (times, depths, temperatures, GPS position, site name) are kept in local storage with your work on that video, and sent to iNaturalist only as part of observations you upload.
  • Frames and GIFs are held in memory and uploaded straight to iNaturalist when you upload an observation.
  • Settings and progress (markers, reviewed sections, sync) stay in local storage. Clear the site's data in your browser to remove them.
  • There are no analytics, cookies or tracking scripts. The page loads the YouTube player, which is covered by Google's privacy policy. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).

The extension

The frame grabber runs only inside YouTube players embedded on the Dive iNat site. It copies frames from the video that's already playing and hands them to the page. It has no network access, stores nothing, and does nothing on any other site, including youtube.com itself.
